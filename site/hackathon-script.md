# Seamstress — 3-minute recorded demo

Record the live site, full screen, one narrator. Talk while you use it. The requirement is a demo of the project in action, with the problem explained, not a pitch read over stills.

Paste this line before you hit record, so typing does not eat the clock:

> A long evening coat with the collar from my first reference and the soft shape of the second.

Have one or two reference photos ready on the desktop.

Say the lines below. The DO lines are what the recording shows. Leave the breaths; they are where the clicks happen.

## 0:00–0:25 — The product, then the problem

DO: Land on the hero. Hold on “Imagine it. Wear it.” Then scroll to “The idea stays a screenshot.” and let that section be read.

SAY: Most of us already have the garment. It is a photo in the camera roll, or a sentence we cannot quite brief. Made-to-measure still asks you to talk like a pattern cutter, so the idea stays a screenshot. The tools that do exist just make another picture: new model, new pose, different clothes every time. Nothing you can hand to someone who sews.

## 0:25–1:05 — Imagine, live

DO: Scroll to “One idea. Six clear steps.” The panel on the right is live. Click the description box and paste the coat line. Add one or two reference photos. Point at them as they appear: shape, detail, fabric, colour. Click “Fuse my idea.”

SAY: This is Seamstress. You start with the idea you already have. Words, or a few references. We fuse them into one garment. The words win, so it stays the coat you meant, not a collage of the photos.

DO: If fusion takes a few seconds, stay quiet on “Cloth finding its shape.” When the picture lands, let it sit for a beat.

SAY: One garment. One direction. That is what a workroom can actually read.

## 1:05–1:20 — Scan, as you scroll

DO: Scroll slowly. Let the panel change to the body scan. Do not pretend you are scanning a person in this take.

SAY: Next, a private scan on your phone. The measurements stay yours, and they are what the fit is built from.

## 1:20–2:00 — Taste, live

DO: Scroll until the slider is under the text, to the left of the garment. Read the axis it chose, Length or Ease. Drag from the left stop to the right stop and back to the one you want. Wait if the end pictures are still arriving, then drag again so the change is visible.

SAY: This is the part you do by hand. One measurement. Shorter or longer, or narrower or roomier. The garment does not turn into a different design. Same fabric, same collar, same closure. Only that decision moves. You are tuning the fit, not prompting a new outfit.

## 2:00–2:15 — Pattern, cut from that fit

DO: Scroll to the pattern. It is cut from the body and the fit you left selected.

SAY: The idea, the body, and that fit become the cutting brief: pieces, grain, and ease, ready for a maker.

## 2:15–2:40 — Maker, then worn, live

DO: Scroll to “Choose your seamstress.” Hold on the pattern, the fabric, and “Send to make.” Then scroll until the worn portrait replaces the panel. She is already dressed in the fit you left selected.

SAY: Then you choose who makes it. She gets the pattern, the brief, and the fabric. Before you send it, you see it worn. Same woman. Same pose. The fit you just left selected. You are judging the clothes, not a new photoshoot.

## 2:40–2:50 — Marketplace

DO: Scroll to “Pass a design on.” If the coat you made is on a card, point at it.

SAY: A design can be handed on, and someone else can make it theirs.

## 2:50–3:00 — Why this, then stop

DO: Stay on the marketplace. Do not cut back to a title card.

SAY: That is the difference. One garment from the references. One measurement at a time. And a person at the end. The model gets you to the workroom. It does not pretend to be the workroom. Seamstress. Imagine it, fit it, and see it worn.

## Before you upload

- Unlisted YouTube or Loom is enough, as long as anyone with the link can watch it without signing in.
- Title: Seamstress — imagine it, fit it, wear it.
- If fuse or “Dressing her…” is slow, pause the recording and resume when the picture is there. Dead waiting time will push you over three minutes.
- Keep the scan and pattern lines short. The moments that have to be live are fuse, the slider, and the worn portrait.
