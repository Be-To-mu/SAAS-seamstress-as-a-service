# Seamstress — Imagine it. Fit it. See it worn.

What if you could turn any idea into a garment made just for you?

A photo from your camera roll. A reference you love. Or simply a description of what you imagine.

No idea is too ambitious.

This is Seamstress.

Start with the idea you already have. Combine words and images to create something uniquely yours.

Shape your design with intuitive tools. Refine the silhouette. Adjust the hemline. Explore colours, materials, and details.

The creative possibilities of a specialist, right at your fingertips.

And when your design feels right, it's time to make it fit.

With a private body scan on your phone, your measurements are transformed into an anonymous digital mannequin, shaped around you.

Your measurements stay yours, becoming the foundation for a garment designed around your body.

See your creation on your own digital twin. Explore the proportions. Refine the details. Imagine how it will look and feel when it's yours.

This is where imagination meets precision.

Behind the scenes, your design and measurements are translated into a 2D pattern, ready for the maker.

Your idea becomes a cutting brief: pattern pieces, grainlines, and ease, prepared for the hands that will bring your garment to life.

Now, choose who makes it.

Discover talented local seamstresses. Explore their previous work, read reviews, and find the maker who feels right for you.

Skilled hands. Hidden talents. A new connection between the people who imagine and the people who create.

You design. We translate and connect. They create.

And your made-to-measure garment arrives at your door.

And when your creation comes to life, why keep it to yourself?

Share your design on the Seamstress marketplace. Let others discover it, make it their own, and have it made.

Your creativity doesn't stop with your own wardrobe. It can inspire someone else's.

Discover new designs, find inspiration, and bring someone else's creation to life.

With Seamstress, every idea has the potential to become something more.
